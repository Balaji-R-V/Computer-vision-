PERSON RECOGNITION

Higher = stricter: fewer people are named, fewer mistakes. 0.72 body strictness is a safe default. If your registration photos come from a different camera or lighting and people are not being recognised, try 0.66, but expect more look-alike mix-ups. The most reliable fix is on the Occupant Time Logs page: use "This is a registered person" on a live occupant so the system learns them from this camera.
